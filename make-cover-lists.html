Make the cover lists

Enter who works which day and in which role. Every shift's cover list is then written for you by the rules, ready to upload as shift_cover_list.csv.

1

Start from

A spreadsheet needs Student, Gender, Shift day and Role columns. Any other columns, such as old cover lists, are ignored. Your work is saved on this device as you go.

2

The shifts

One line for each shift. A student who works three days has three lines. Gender is used only for the Buckets & Composting rule (women only). It never appears in the app.

    The cover lists

    For each shift: everyone not working that day, those with the fewest shifts first (ties in alphabetical order). Only women are listed for Buckets & Composting.

      5

      Download and upload

      Then put it in the app's files on GitHub, replacing the old one:
      1. Open the app's files and click Add file → Upload files.
      2. Drag in shift_cover_list.csv. If your computer renamed it, for example to shift_cover_list (1).csv, rename it back first.
      3. Click Commit changes, wait about 2 minutes, then check the bottom of the app.
      The full steps are in HOW-TO-UPDATE.md, step 4.
